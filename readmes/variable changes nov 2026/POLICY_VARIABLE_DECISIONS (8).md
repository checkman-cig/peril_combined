# Policy Variable Cleaning & Aggregation Log

**Last updated:** 2026-11-02  
**Scope:** Existing fields in `src/build_policy.py`: 28 `POLICY_YEAR_VALUE_COLS` (including one planned removal) and 13 `POLICY_LOCATION_COLS`. Do not add variables from Oracle during this review.

## Purpose and conventions

This is the running source of truth **for planned policy-variable data cleaning, term aggregation, and annual-vs-location treatment**. It records decisions from the policy/claims review; it does not replace `MODELING_RULES.md` for modeling methodology or `MODEL_STATUS.md` for model state.

**Statuses (decision readiness, not implementation):**

- **Finalized:** Treatment has been agreed. No further review planned without new evidence.
- **In Review:** Proposed treatment exists; a check or decision is still outstanding.
- **Needs Investigation:** Coding, data quality, or interpretation is not yet understood well enough to select treatment.

**Implementation:** No production modifications from this review have been applied. `build_policy_year()` currently uses `last` for annual fields; `build_policy_location()` selects a latest dated property record. Entries below describe **planned** treatment, not current output.

**Shared aggregation principles:** For future numeric weighted means and categorical weighted modes, use **unrounded term-level exposure** (`num_months`) as the weight, then retain existing final exposure rounding. A weighted mode sums exposure over terms with equal categories. Tie-breaking and how to handle NULL versus known values within the same year **remain to be finalized**. Keep `dec_policy` as an identifier, outside this 41-field inventory.

## Evidence and interpretation notes

- Source code: `src/build_policy.py` and `sql/pull_policy.sql` from `peril_combined_code6.zip`.
- SQL quality audits: `POLICY_PERIL_DEDUP` (Homeowner: 2,212,607 declaration rows; Dwelling Fire: 919,686). These are **rows**, not unique policy-years. The categorical frequency audit also used this table, grouped by `business_line`.
- Location-change audit denominators: 289,766 HOC and 116,974 DFC buildings with multiple term dates. Differences are preliminary; date ties and normalization can affect comparisons.
- Square-footage audit: unusually low Coverage A per square foot above 20,000 sq. ft.; exceptionally high Coverage A per square foot below 300 for primary dwelling forms. Within 2012–2025 overlapping terms, out-of-range values appeared in 308 building/band observations at 0–299 (HOC 117, DFC 191) and 92 at 20,000+ (HOC 80, DFC 12). These counts are by band, not property-year, and should not be assumed mutually exclusive.
- `residence_type` audit: Expected Primary/Secondary/Seasonal labels by policy form. Full-history missingness estimates differed from the 2012–2025 overlapping-term audit: DFC-3 35.56% NULL and 2.29% Secondary/Seasonal; HOC-3 51.96% NULL and 1.05% Secondary/Seasonal; HOC-4 12.54% NULL; HOC-5 15.59% NULL; HOC-6 24.54% NULL and 5.26% Secondary/Seasonal. Consecutive-term change query returned no category or NULL/known transitions. User revised the finalized decision on 2026-11-02 to latest recorded value in `policy_location` rather than annual exposure-weighted mode; not yet implemented.
- `occupant` audit: Six non-NULL categories observed; keep rare Vacant, Unoccupied, and Course of Construction labels separate. In 2012-2025 active terms, DFC-3 was 64.23% Tenant and 35.56% NULL; HOC-3 47.89% Owner and 51.96% NULL; HOC-4 83.13% Tenant and 12.54% NULL; HOC-5 84.10% Owner and 15.59% NULL; HOC-6 50.35% Owner, 24.98% Tenant, 24.57% NULL. Prior full-history missingness was higher. No consecutive-term category or NULL/known changes were detected in 2012-2025. **Finalized revision 2026-11-02:** retain as latest recorded `policy_location` value, not annual weighted mode; test incremental contribution versus `policy_form` later. No production changes.
- `occupancy` audit: DFC has only Owner/Tenant, HOC is wholly NULL; 2012–2025 saw 203 occupancy transitions across 202 buildings. Subsequent-term review showed no immediate reversions, 169 with the new value also at the last record, 33 without subsequent data, and one later reversal. The last-record check cannot exclude intervening reversions. This supports annual treatment, not a static location value.
- `nbr_of_families` audit: DFC contains only `1 Family` through `4 Families`, with no missing values; HOC wholly NULL. There were 173 known-to-known term transitions during 2012–2025. Preserve distinct categories and year-specific changes; annual mode is finalized but not yet implemented.

DFC observed plausible counts 0–43, no observations from 44 to 100, and obvious outliers from 1,440 to 119,415,491; 11 records for one DFC building report -3. In the 2012–2025 DFC-3 latest-building snapshot, 48.13% were zero, 26.31% NULL and 25.54% positive (1–100), so the feature is not near-constant among DFC buildings. Across 2012–2025, 72,202 HOC and 36,204 DFC transitions occurred, with approximately 65% of HOC and 63% of DFC transitions concentrated in 2015–2017. The coordinated shift suggests possible classification methodology changes, not confirmed changes in property fire protection. Annual categorical weighted mode is finalized, with original labels preserved until existing downstream numerical mapping. No production code changes.

- `central_alarm` audit: Only 0/1 when non-NULL; HOC has no missing values, DFC-3 about 3.41% missing. Preserve all values distinctly; no cleaning needed and separate predictive value testing remains out of scope.
- `local_fire` audit: DFC-3 has only 0 and 1 (75.26% of rows are 1); HOC is 100% NULL by source design. In 2012–2025, DFC transitions were 148 from 0→1 and 15 from 1→0. Preserve HOC structural NULL; planned annual weighted mode is finalized, but not implemented.
- Construction-year audit: 0/negative and impossible years observed; five DFC buildings with reported years below 1800 had terms overlapping 2012–2025 (187, 199, 940, 1690, 1765). Of the two investigated older-year buildings, one reported 1690 only and the other recorded both 1765 and 1981. Year 1900 showed a strong spike (4,463 HOC and 4,904 DFC buildings ever reporting it); it may be an approximate default but remains useful as an old-age indicator. Year 2000 was not anomalous relative to adjacent years. Same-date construction-year conflict query returned no rows.
- `ho_fire_station_miles` evidence: Zero is widespread but varies substantially by policy form and state (DFC-3 WA 94.0%, OR 60.3%, CA 32.7% in the 2012–2025 active-building snapshot). Extreme positive values include 900 (Port Angeles), 630 (Brisbane), and 500 (several California cities), often with protection class 1–4. **Finalized removal** from selected pipeline fields; the previous 0 -> NULL and >=50 -> NULL proposals are superseded. No model-ablation evidence was collected. The distinct declaration-level `fire_station_miles` field is now finalized as a two-category location-level distance variable.
- Declaration fire-protection distance finalization (2026-11-02): the parameter table confirms 266046/266047 as <=5/>5 station miles and 266044/266045 as <=1000/>1000 hydrant feet. Raw station mile values were 0 (10 rows, 8 DFC buildings), 1, 1.5, 2, 3, 4, and 5; 5 appeared in 11,208 rows. Numeric 5 preceded code 266046 in 3,062 buildings and 266047 in only 9; numeric 0 preceded 266046 in 7 buildings but is treated as NULL because its meaning is ambiguous. Raw hydrant 500 feet occurred in 6 rows / 2 buildings, with one later 266044 record. Direct coded-category changes (station 77 DFC + 53 HOC; hydrant 159 DFC + 793 HOC) were uncommon and mainly in 2015–2016, consistent with possible rating recategorization; latest location-level values are adopted.
- NULL percentages in fields applicable only to one business line are not by themselves defects. Large numerical values and thresholds flagged during audits require interpretation before any automatic replacement.
